# Example 1: Green-flag listing (worth pursuing)

This is a synthetic example showing what a legitimate, well-priced
private-party listing looks like and how the skill should evaluate it.

## Listing card

```
2010 Honda Accord EX-L Sedan
$5,800 (was $6,400)
112,000 mi
Posted 3 weeks ago in Springfield, IL
```

## Listing body text

> Selling my 2010 Accord EX-L 4-cyl. Bought it from my dad in 2018 when
> he stopped driving; he was the original owner. Clean title, never any
> accidents, never smoked in.
>
> Leather seats (driver's side has some wear on the bolster, otherwise
> nice). Sunroof works. Heated seats work. AC blows cold.
>
> Maintenance: oil changes every 5K at the dealership through 2020, then
> at the indie shop down the street. Front brake pads and rotors done
> April 2024, $480. New battery November 2023. Timing belt is N/A on
> this engine (K24, timing chain). Transmission fluid done at 90K. Set
> of Continental TrueContact tires installed 6 months ago.
>
> Selling because we just had a baby and need something bigger. Cash
> only, will accept cashier's check from a local bank if we meet there.
> Title in my name, lien-free.
>
> Happy to answer questions and let you get a PPI at any shop you pick.
> Will provide the VIN to anyone serious.

## Seller profile (what you'd see clicking through)

- Profile: Sarah Chen
- Joined Facebook: 2013
- Marketplace ratings: 4 (all positive, all from non-vehicle sales over
  the last 5 years)
- Active listings: this Accord + a stroller
- Recently sold: a high chair, two pieces of furniture
- Timeline: family photos, occasional posts about her kid

## How the skill should evaluate this

```
VERDICT: TIER 1
Year/Make/Model/Trim: 2010 Honda Accord EX-L 4-cyl (verify trim from
  interior photos: leather + heated seats + sunroof checks out)
Ask: $5,800 / Estimated fair private-party: $5,500 to $6,800
Engine: K24A8 2.4L I4, timing CHAIN, no scheduled belt service
Known issues for this gen: 2008 to 2012 K24 has an oil-burn TSB
  pattern; ask seller about oil consumption between changes
Seller signal: GREEN. Established account, 1 active vehicle, real
  personal narrative ("dad stopped driving, just had a baby"), specific
  dated maintenance with dollar amounts and receipts implied
Body-text flags: none. Honest cosmetic disclosure (seat bolster wear),
  specific dated services, clean title stated, willing to provide VIN
  and allow PPI
Deferred maintenance risk: LOW. Tires recent. Brakes recent. Battery
  recent. Trans fluid done. Check coolant and spark plugs at PPI;
  factor those if overdue
Negotiation lever: 4-cyl Accord EX-L 2010 with 112K is right at fair
  market. Lever is the seat bolster wear and any PPI findings; not
  much room. Likely lands at $5,400 to $5,700.
Next step: Ask seller about oil consumption history. Request VIN, pull
  Carfax (~$40). Schedule PPI at a Honda-specialist indie ($150).
```

## Why this is a good listing

- Seller profile is consistent with a real private sale (established
  account, single vehicle listing, family timeline)
- Body text has specific dated maintenance with receipts implied
- Honest cosmetic disclosure (bolster wear)
- Acknowledges the K24 timing-chain fact correctly
- Personal narrative for the sale (had a baby)
- Welcomes PPI and VIN sharing
- Price reduced once already, motivated but not desperate

The skill should walk the user to a verdict in under 60 seconds.
