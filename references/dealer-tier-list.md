## How the tiers map to scoring

- **Tier A — ≤ 60 mi.** Full scoring credit. Easy day-of PPI, easy
  walk-away, direct visit feasible same week.
- **Tier B — 60 to 150 mi.** Full scoring credit. Single-day PPI
  round-trip; pickup or transport same day.
- **Tier C — 150 to 250 mi.** **Escalation only.** Listing must be
  visibly exceptional on price, options, or CPO status to merit
  pursuit. Flatbed transport ($800 to $1,800) priced in by default
  on the negotiation brief.
- **> 250 mi:** auto-**Pass** per `CONTEXT.md` geography rule.

## Status legend

- **VERIFIED** — confirmed on MBUSA dealer locator; basic reputation
  checked.
- **TO-VERIFY** — name and location drafted from public knowledge;
  confirm against `https://www.mbusa.com/en/dealers` before using the
  entry to score live candidates.
- **KNOWN-GOOD** — prior direct interaction; reputation confirmed.
- **AVOID** — prior negative interaction or documented pattern issue;
  treat any listing from this dealer as auto-Pass regardless of price.

## Notes on use

- **MBUSA inventory locator** sometimes surfaces inventory from
  dealers not on this list (dealer-to-dealer transfers, recently
  flipped franchises). When an alert comes from an unlisted dealer,
  add a TO-VERIFY entry here in the correct tier before scoring the
  candidate.
- **Distance figures are driving-distance estimates**, not straight-
  line. Round-trip *drive time* is the operational constraint —
  anything past Baltimore on a Friday afternoon during DC rush is
  effectively Tier C. The skill should ask for the user's preferred
  travel window when distance is borderline.
- **Tier C transport math:** assume $1.50 to $2.50 per loaded mile for
  open enclosed transport from a regional broker; door-to-door, 7 to
  14 day window. Faster (3 to 5 day) is +30 to 50%. Quote two
  carriers when a Tier C candidate clears scoring.
