# Identifying trim from photos and Carfax

Trim level can swing the fair-private-party value of a 10-year-old
mainstream sedan by $1,500. Sellers misreport trim on the listing card
about 20% of the time, usually upward (claiming EX-L when it's an LX).
Always verify trim before committing to a valuation.

## What's in the Carfax

The Carfax decodes the VIN to a base trim string, but on most US-market
cars the VIN does NOT encode the full trim level. VIN positions 4
through 8 vary by manufacturer; many do not encode trim suffix at all.
The Carfax often shows just "Honda Accord 4DR Sedan" or "Toyota Camry
SE Sedan," and the trim suffix may be missing or wrong.

Honda, Toyota, Mazda, and Subaru: VIN encodes base model + body style but
usually not trim suffix.
BMW, Mercedes, Audi: VIN encodes more, sometimes including engine.
Ford, GM: variable; sometimes encodes trim, sometimes not.

**Rule:** do not trust the Carfax trim suffix. Verify from photos.

## Where to look in photos

The fastest tells, in order of usefulness:

1. **Wheels.** Steel wheels with plastic hubcaps = base or low trim.
   Painted alloys = mid trim. Polished alloys / two-tone alloys / larger
   sizes = top trim or sport package.
2. **Seats.** Cloth = base/mid. Leather (or leatherette) = top trim or
   leather package. Power adjustments / heated seat buttons = top trim.
3. **Steering wheel.** Bare wheel with no buttons = base. Cruise-control
   buttons only = mid. Audio + phone + paddle shifters = top.
4. **Center stack.** Single-line monochrome radio = base. Multi-line
   monochrome = low/mid. Color touchscreen = mid/top depending on year.
   Built-in navigation screen with map = nav package.
5. **Climate controls.** 3-knob manual = base/mid. Single-zone auto = mid.
   Dual-zone auto with display = top.
6. **Headlights.** Halogen reflector = base/mid. Projector = mid. HID or
   LED with auto-leveling = top.
7. **Exterior trim.** Black plastic bumper inserts = base. Body-colored =
   mid/top. Chrome window trim = mid/top. Roof rails or sunroof = top.
8. **Badge on trunk lid or fender.** Trim badge is the most direct
   evidence but is often swapped by sellers ("EX badge" on an LX car).
   Use only as a corroborating signal.

## Brand-specific trim ladders (2005 to 2020 range)

### Honda

| Model | Trim ladder (low to high) |
|---|---|
| Civic | DX -> LX -> EX -> EX-L -> Si (sport) -> Touring (post-2015) |
| Accord | LX -> Sport -> EX -> EX-L -> Touring |
| CR-V | LX -> EX -> EX-L -> Touring |
| Fit | Base -> Sport -> EX -> EX-L |

Civic DX note: AC was optional, not standard, on the DX pre-2006.

| Trim | Visual tells |
|---|---|
| LX | Steel wheels with plastic covers, cloth, single-DIN radio, bare wheel (cruise on column stalk pre-2013) |
| EX | Alloy wheels, sunroof, cruise on steering wheel, premium cloth |
| EX-L | Leather, heated seats, possibly nav, alloy wheels |

### Toyota

| Model | Trim ladder (low to high) |
|---|---|
| Corolla (modern) | L -> LE -> S -> XLE -> XSE |
| Corolla (2003 to 2008) | CE -> S -> LE -> XRS |
| Camry | L -> LE -> SE -> XLE -> XSE (CE / Standard for older) |
| Avalon (3rd gen, 2005 to 2012) | XL -> Touring -> XLS -> Limited |
| Avalon (4th gen, 2013 to 2018) | XLE -> XLE Premium -> Touring -> Limited |
| RAV4 | LE -> XLE -> SE -> Limited -> Platinum |

| Trim | Visual tells |
|---|---|
| LE | Steel wheels or basic alloys, cloth, single-DIN radio |
| SE | Sport bumpers, leatherette, paddle shifters on automatic |
| XLE / XSE / Limited | Leather, moonroof, premium audio, smart-key |

### Mazda

| Model | Trim ladder (low to high) |
|---|---|
| Mazda3 | i Sport -> i Touring -> i Grand Touring -> s Grand Touring |
| Mazda6 | i Sport -> i Touring -> i Grand Touring -> s Grand Touring |
| CX-5 | Sport -> Touring -> Grand Touring -> Signature |

Mazda3/6 "s" prefix = larger engine pre-2014.

Grand Touring tells: leather, Bose audio, sunroof, alloy wheels with
unique finish, color screen.

### Subaru

Impreza / Legacy / Outback / Forester all share the same ladder:
Base -> Premium -> Sport -> Limited -> Touring (and XT for turbo on
some).

| Trim | Visual tells |
|---|---|
| Base | Steel wheels, manual everything |
| Premium | Alloys, cloth, some power features |
| Limited | Leather, heated seats, moonroof, alloys, nav |

### Lexus

Lexus models (ES, IS, GS, RX) historically have only one or two
trim levels per generation, distinguished by option packages. "350"
vs "300h" is engine, not trim. Look for option packages on the window
sticker or build sheet: Premium / Luxury / Ultra Luxury, Navigation,
F-Sport, Cold-weather.

### Acura

Almost all Acuras come in two flavors: "base" and "with Tech Package"
(or Advance, A-Spec). The Tech Package adds nav, ELS premium audio,
heated/ventilated seats. Look for the nav screen and ELS speakers.

## Sequence for identifying trim from a listing

When the user pastes photos:

1. **Skim the listing-card trim claim** but treat it as a hint, not
   truth.
2. **Look at the wheels.** Steel + hubcap caps the trim ceiling at low/mid.
3. **Look at the center stack and steering wheel.** Single-line radio
   and bare wheel: base. Multi-button wheel and color screen: mid/top.
4. **Look at the seats.** Leather: confirms mid/top. Cloth: caps at mid
   for most brands.
5. **Cross-reference with the trim ladder for that model/year above.**
6. **Confirm with the user**: "Based on the photos this looks like an
   [LX]. The steel wheels rule out EX. The single-DIN radio rules out
   EX-L. Can you confirm there's no leather visible in the other photos?"
7. **Revise valuation based on confirmed trim**, not the seller's claim.

## When in doubt, ask for specific photos

If the photos are ambiguous, ask the seller for:

- Close-up of the driver's seat from outside the open door
- Photo of the center stack with the radio on
- Photo of the steering wheel head-on
- Photo of the wheel up close

Real owners send these without complaint. Curbstoners stall.

## Special cases

- **Sport packages on base trims**: some manufacturers offered a Sport
  package on the base trim (e.g., Honda Civic LX-S). This is rare but
  real. Look for body-kit pieces and unique wheels.
- **Dealer-added options**: a base car with leather seat covers, an
  aftermarket head unit, and dealer-installed mudflaps can look mid-trim
  at a glance. Look at OEM-specific tells (heated seat buttons, factory
  steering-wheel controls) rather than the visible interior.
- **Re-trimmed or re-badged cars**: rare but exists, especially with
  totaled higher-trim cars donating parts to lower-trim cars. Mismatched
  components (steering wheel from EX, dash from LX) are a flag.

## Why this matters for valuation

Trim affects KBB value by:

- $500 to $1,500 on compact cars (Civic, Corolla, Mazda3)
- $1,500 to $3,000 on midsize (Accord, Camry, Mazda6)
- $2,000 to $5,000 on luxury or SUV (Lexus, Acura, RX, MDX)

A seller listing as EX-L with photos that show an LX is asking the EX-L
price. If you confirm LX from the photos, the negotiation lever is
right there.
